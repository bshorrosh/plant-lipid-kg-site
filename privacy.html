Privacy

Anyone can sign in with Google to view the site; invited reviewers can also add notes. When you sign in, the site receives your email address from Google. It uses it to check whether you're on the reviewer list and to mark which notes are yours. Your password stays with Google, and the site has no access to anything else in your Google account.

The project records your email address and when you open the site, to know who has looked at it. These records are kept privately by the project.

If you're a reviewer, your notes, with your email address and dates, are kept with the project's review notes, and other reviewers can see them. Images you attach to a note are kept in the project's private Google Drive folder, not shared by link. Other reviewers see them with your note, and only you can remove them.

Your browser remembers that you're signed in for 30 days; Sign out ends that. If you use Chat, your API key and questions go straight from your browser to the AI service you chose.

There's no advertising or tracking, and nothing is shared outside the project. For any question, or to have your records or notes removed, contact Basil Shorrosh.
