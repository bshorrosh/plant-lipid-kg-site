Privacy

The review page is for invited reviewers. When you sign in with Google, the page receives your email address, which it uses to check that you're on the reviewer list and to mark which notes are yours. Your password stays with Google, and the page has no access to anything else in your Google account.

Your notes, with your email address and dates, are kept with the project's review notes, and other reviewers can see them. The project also notes when the review page is opened, to know who has had a chance to look.

Your browser remembers that you're signed in for 30 days; Sign out ends that. If you use Chat, your API key and questions go straight from your browser to the AI service you chose.

There's no advertising or tracking, and nothing is shared outside the review. For any question, or to have your notes removed, contact the person who invited you.
