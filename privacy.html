Privacy

The site is for invited reviewers, and may later open to other visitors. When you sign in with Google, the site receives your email address, which it uses to check whether you're on the reviewer list (only reviewers can add notes) and to mark which notes are yours. Your password stays with Google, and the page has no access to anything else in your Google account.

Your notes, with your email address and dates, are kept with the project's review notes, and other reviewers can see them. The project also notes when the site is opened, to know who has had a chance to look.

Images you attach to a note are kept in the project's private Google Drive folder, not shared by link. Other reviewers see them with your note, and only you can remove them.

Your browser remembers that you're signed in for 30 days; Sign out ends that. If you use Chat, your API key and questions go straight from your browser to the AI service you chose.

There's no advertising or tracking, and nothing is shared outside the review. For any question, or to have your notes removed, contact Basil Shorrosh or the person who invited you.
